DevKraken iOS Apps

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to iOS applications published under the DevKraken name, unless an individual app provides its own separate privacy policy.

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Information We Collect

DevKraken apps are designed to collect only the information required to provide the app's functionality. Unless a specific app screen or App Store listing states otherwise, we do not collect personal information directly from users.

Some apps may store preferences, settings, progress, or app-generated content locally on your device. This information is used only to operate the app experience and is not sent to DevKraken unless the app clearly provides a feature that requires transmission.

Analytics, Diagnostics, and App Store Data

Apple may provide developers with aggregated analytics, crash reports, diagnostics, and App Store performance information. This information is generally not used to identify individual users and is handled according to Apple's own privacy practices.

Tracking and Advertising

DevKraken does not sell personal information and does not use personal information from DevKraken iOS apps to track users across apps or websites owned by other companies. If an individual app includes advertising or third-party tracking, that app's App Store privacy details or separate policy will disclose it.

Third-Party Services

Some apps may use Apple services or other third-party services for features such as cloud storage, authentication, purchases, notifications, maps, or crash reporting. When those services are used, their privacy policies and terms may also apply.

Children's Privacy

DevKraken apps are not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a DevKraken app, contact us so we can review and address the request.

Data Retention

Information stored locally by an app remains on your device until you delete it through the app, remove the app, or erase the device data. If a specific app transmits information to a DevKraken-controlled service, we retain it only as long as needed to provide the feature, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or maintain security.

Your Choices

You can manage app permissions such as notifications, location, camera, photos, and other device access from iOS Settings. You can also delete an app to remove locally stored app data from your device.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our apps, services, or legal requirements change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised last updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions about DevKraken iOS apps, contact us at hola@devkraken.com.
