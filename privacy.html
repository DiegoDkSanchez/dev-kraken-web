Privacy Policy for iOS Apps

This Privacy Policy applies to iOS applications published under the DevKraken name, unless an individual app provides its own separate privacy policy.

DevKraken iOS apps are designed to collect only the information required to provide the app's functionality. Unless a specific app screen or App Store listing states otherwise, we do not collect personal information directly from users.

Some apps may store preferences, settings, progress, or app-generated content locally on your device. This information is used only to operate the app experience and is not sent to DevKraken unless the app clearly provides a feature that requires transmission.

DevKraken does not sell personal information and does not use personal information from DevKraken iOS apps to track users across apps or websites owned by other companies. Third-Party Services

Some apps may use Apple services or other third-party services for features such as cloud storage, authentication, purchases, notifications, maps, or crash reporting. When those services are used, their privacy policies and terms may also apply.

DevKraken iOS apps covered by this generic policy are not directed to children and are not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Information stored locally by an app remains on your device until you delete it through the app, remove the app, or erase the device data. If a specific app transmits information to a DevKraken-controlled service, we retain it only as long as needed to provide the feature, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or maintain security.

Your Choices

You can manage app permissions such as notifications, location, camera, photos, and other device access from iOS Settings. You can also delete an app to remove locally stored app data from your device.
